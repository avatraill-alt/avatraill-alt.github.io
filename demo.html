1. What is an informed user?

Cats are observant animals. Before choosing a sunny windowsill, a cat often checks the warmth, the view, and whether the spot is already occupied. This small routine is a good example of gathering information before making a choice.

In my example, the cat uses what it notices to select the most comfortable place for an afternoon nap.

2. Do you want to be an informed user?

When buying eggs, I would want to know useful details such as the price, the date on the carton, and how many eggs are included. Those details make it easier to choose the carton that fits my needs.

Being informed does not mean knowing everything; it means paying attention to the facts that matter for a decision.

3. What does “constant call” mean?

A cat waiting beside an automatic feeder can seem as if it is on constant call: it listens for every small sound and appears immediately when it expects food. Of course, this is only a playful example of being ready to respond at any moment.

4. Viewpoint: the employer

From the egg carton’s point of view, organization matters. Each egg needs a steady place so it can be protected during the trip home. The carton would likely value reliability and careful handling because both help keep the eggs safe.

5. Viewpoint: the employee on call

From the cat’s point of view, responding to every rustle in the kitchen could become tiring. A cat may enjoy being alert, but it also needs quiet time, naps, and space away from the noise.
